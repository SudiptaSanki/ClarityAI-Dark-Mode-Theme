About This Test Article

Artificial Intelligence architecture has shifted rapidly towards decentralized provider ecosystems. Where developers once depended on single proprietary endpoints with rigid quotas and deprecation cycles, modern applications require total provider and model agnosticism.

ClarityAI empowers users to connect any AI model from any provider—including Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, DeepSeek, or self-hosted local LLMs like Ollama—without changing a single line of extension code.

Strategic Advantages of Multi-Model Architecture

  • Zero Vendor Lock-in: Switch from Gemini to Groq (Llama 3.3 70B) or DeepSeek instantaneously whenever quotas or pricing fluctuate.
  • Complete Cost Control: Non-coders can easily choose free API keys (Google AI Studio, Groq Console, OpenRouter Free) or plug in high-tier flagship keys as needed.
  • Future-Proof Model Flexibility: Never be blocked by hardcoded model versions. When a new model releases (e.g. Gemini 3, GPT-5, Llama 4), users simply type the model string in Settings.
  • Executive Consultation Output: Tailor the analytical output into TL;DR Executive Briefs, Structured Bullets, 4-Part Deep Dive Consultations, or Actionable Checklists.
  • Professional Artifact Export: Export your generated intelligence directly to a styled, clean PDF or copy clean text/markdown with one click.

Step-by-Step Extension Testing Checklist

  1. Configure Provider: Click the ClarityAI icon in the browser toolbar, open Settings, and choose a provider (e.g., Groq with a free key, or Google Gemini).
  2. Test Connection: Hit ⚡ Test Connection to confirm your key and model are authenticated.
  3. Select Consultation Style: Try switching between Executive Brief, Key Takeaways (Bullets), and Deep Dive Consultation.
  4. Summarize This Page: Click ✨ Summarize. Confirm that the summary renders with clean bold text and bullet points.
  5. Interactive Follow-Up: In the bottom consultation bar, ask: "What are the main advantages of multi-model architecture?" to test interactive Q&A.
  6. Export PDF & Copy: Click 📄 Export PDF to download a clean brief, and 📋 Copy All to copy the formatted text.
